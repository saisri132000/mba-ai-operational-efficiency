Evidence and boundaries

The source study covers Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, the United Kingdom, and the United States, in finance and insurance and manufacturing. These are genuine published OECD survey aggregates, not responses collected by this project.

Results do not represent Chennai or India, a current adoption census, measured financial savings, or causal effects. Employer and worker populations are separate and unpaired.

A finance staff-cost motivation distribution in Figure 3.1 totals 94%. It is disclosed and excluded from comparisons; it is not selectable in this dashboard.

Source: Lane, Williams, and Broecke (2023), The impact of AI on the workplace, OECD Working Paper No. 288, and its supplementary workbook. Read the original OECD report.
